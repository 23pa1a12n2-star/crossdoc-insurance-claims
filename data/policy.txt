# MOTOR INSURANCE POLICY TERMS AND CONDITIONS

## Section 1: Coverage Definitions
1.1 Comprehensively covers damage to the insured vehicle caused by accidental collision, theft, or malicious acts.

## Section 2: Claim Filing Windows
2.1 All accident and damage claims must be formally reported to the company within thirty (30) days of the incident occurrence date.

## Section 3: Required Documentation
3.1 Every claim requires a formal Claim Form and a Customer Incident Description.
3.2 Claims specifically relating to Theft or Malicious Damage strictly require a First Information Report (FIR) from the police jurisdiction where the incident occurred.
3.3 Claims relating to Accidental Collision require a Repair Estimate from a certified garage.

## Section 4: General Exclusions
4.1 Commercial Use Exclusion: Coverage is entirely invalidated if the insured vehicle is being used for hire, reward, ridesharing, package delivery, or any other commercial enterprise at the time of the incident.
4.2 Racing Exclusion: Coverage is entirely invalidated if the vehicle is operating on a racetrack, engaging in speed testing, or participating in organized competitive driving.
4.3 Intoxication Exclusion: The company is not liable for accidents occurring while the driver is under the influence of alcohol or restricted substances.
